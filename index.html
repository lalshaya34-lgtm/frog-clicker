🐸 How To Play

Welcome to your living frog pond. Click the frog to make it stick out its tongue and collect Lilypad Coins.

👅 Clicking

Every click generates coins and XP. Rapid clicks create a combo multiplier. Critical hits can produce much larger rewards.

🪰 Living Pond

Flies, butterflies, fireflies, ripples, reeds, lily pads, weather and day/night effects continuously animate the pond.

🧬 300 Mechanics

There are 30 upgrade families with 10 levels each. The mechanics affect click power, passive income, luck, critical hits, XP, weather and more.

🎨 Skins

Unlock frog skins by accumulating lifetime coins. The final skin is the ☠️ Poison Dart Frog.

⚡ Energy

There is NO ENERGY SYSTEM. Click as much as you want.
